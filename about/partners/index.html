Working alongside trusted partners

Lasting change is built through collaboration. Sembrandopaz works alongside community organizations, universities, faith-based institutions, government agencies, and international partners who share our commitment to peacebuilding, environmental conservation, sustainable development, and community well-being throughout Montes de María. Together, these partnerships strengthen our ability to create lasting impact.
