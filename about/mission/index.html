Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

We are a community that respects human dignity and engages in creative transformation to become a restorative community space. We engage communities through the values of participatory education, nonviolent coexistence, spirituality, solidarity, dialogue, democracy and respect for the environment. We strive to generate “wealth” in a broad sense, through holistic human development that allows individuals to contribute to a more just and harmonious society.

Vision

We see ourselves as a sustainable community that serves as an inspiration, an example and a support network for other communities, helping them build sustainability through new forms of integration and collective efforts to overcome ignorance, inequality, poverty, and hunger.

Values

Sembrandopaz aims to be a facilitating space that supports the efforts of grassroots civil society organizations to build a sustainable culture of peace through social justice and sustainable human development, serving as an educational bridge between the population and state institutions to strengthen the public sector.
