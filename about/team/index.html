Organizational structure

Sembrandopaz is legally organized as an Association. It includes a General Assembly of Associates, which is the maximum authority of the organization and meets annually. The Board of Directors is the head of the Assembly, assuring the implementation of organizational policies and agreements. The Sembrandopaz team has the responsibility to carry out the work of the organization according to the mission and vision, and the direction of its executive and operational leadership.
